## English

Adds bounded Fullbright diagnostics for the healing-related first-person transition: eligibility, light-target loss/recovery, failure reasons and consumer call/application counts are recorded in the existing Native log.

Lighting behavior remains based on 0.1.12. Camera/FOV controls from 0.1.15 are retained. **The healing-related darkening is still unresolved; this release collects evidence for the next fix.**

Download **RE7_OTS_Native_v0.1.16_SteamDeck.zip** and follow the included English or Korean guide. To capture the issue, start a fresh session with Fullbright and third person enabled, wait 5 seconds, heal once, wait 5 seconds after third person returns, then exit and keep `native_*.jsonl` and `reframework.log` from that session.

Exit the game before updating; for script-managed installs, run the installed uninstaller first. Settings, logs and models are preserved. The original CT and third-party models are not included.

## 한국어

밝기 동작은 0.1.12 기준을 유지하며 0.1.15의 카메라·FOV 설정도 포함합니다. **치료 중 어두워지는 문제는 아직 미해결이며, 이번 버전은 다음 수정을 위한 근거를 수집합니다.**
